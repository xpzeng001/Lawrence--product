## Style Prompt

Warm legal-tech workstation. A MacBook Pro anchors the scene while identity cards appear as official employee provisioning: work computer, email account, Feishu account, workspace, task system, and multi-channel access. The shot should feel precise, credible, premium, and operational.

## Typography

Use Inter, SF Pro Display, PingFang SC, Noto Sans SC, Microsoft YaHei, and system sans-serif fallbacks. Large Chinese display text should be bold, steady, and readable; interface labels should be compact and high-contrast.

## Motion

The laptop opens first. The dashboard activates next. Identity cards then arrive one by one, followed by multi-channel access and the final screen text. Motion is restrained: short slides, small scale changes, precise connector draws, and one soft light sweep.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use neon blue, purple AI gradients, or dark cyberpunk styling.
- Do not crowd the composition with tiny UI text.
- Do not make the laptop feel decorative; it is the work identity anchor.
- Do not use random or infinite animation.
